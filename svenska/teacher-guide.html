Guide

A reference for using the Dialog Session page.

Roles

Teacher Sees the entire dialog. Controls what the student sees.
Student Sees only what the teacher reveals.

Default visibility

The student sees their own lines (whichever speaker — A or B — is assigned to them) in plain English. The other speaker's lines appear as placeholder bubbles. The Swedish translations are hidden on both sides until the teacher reveals them.

(Swedish hidden)
Yes, I'm making carrot cake.
(Swedish hidden)
(Swedish hidden)
Thanks!
(Swedish hidden)

Controls

Tap coloured EN bubble Toggles whether the student sees the English text for that line.
Tap grey SV bubble Toggles whether the student sees the Swedish translation for that line.
Reveal next →  (or space) Steps through the dialog linearly: line 1 EN → line 1 SV → line 2 EN → …
shift + space Step back — hides the most-recently-revealed bubble.
Reveal all / Hide all Flips every line at once.
Show all to student Override — everything is visible to the student regardless of per-line state.
Strict Hides the student's own lines too. The student sees only what the teacher reveals.
Student speaks as: A / B Assigns which speaker (A in blue or B in green) is the student.
Next dialog → Generates a new random dialog.
← Prev Returns to the previous dialog.

Suggested flow

  1. A random dialog appears.
  2. The teacher reads their line out loud and taps it to reveal it on the student's screen.
  3. The student responds in Swedish. The teacher taps to reveal the model Swedish for comparison.
  4. Continue line by line. When done, tap Reveal all for review, then Next dialog.
Some dialogs are intentionally a bit absurd. The aim is grammar reflex, not realism.
